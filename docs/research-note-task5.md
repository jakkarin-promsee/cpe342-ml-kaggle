## 15 November: peer-relative change and machine-like regularity hypotheses (v3 EDA, v7 models)

The full Task 5 situation suggests two **separate** detection hypotheses. H1 asks whether unusual change relative to an account's own four-period behavior and to peers with similar baseline behavior improves the frozen v17 ranking. H2 asks whether sustained activity together with unusually mechanical regularity adds information beyond H1. The narrative examples (theft, account selling, bot farming, VPN pricing) are **not** four observed target labels or known score weights. The datacard says `_1`–`_4` are consecutive periods but does not identify the newest endpoint, so v3's new change features are direction agnostic. Four observations do not support a reliable per-account variance estimate on their own.

`src/task5/eda/build_eda_behavior_v3.ipynb` reads the raw 25,889-row test-only file, masks out-of-range coordinate pairs using the v2 rule, and saves exactly one dataset, `eda_behavior_views_v3`, with **122 raw + 45 new = 167 predictors**. The exact new names/patterns are:

- For each of `login_count`, `location_changes`, `device_count`, `ip_hash_entropy`, `login_time_variance`, `session_duration_mean`, `play_pattern_consistency`, `activity_level`, `skill_rating`, `reaction_time_mean`, `purchase_frequency`, `purchase_amount_mean`, `currency_transfers`, `communication_frequency`: `<family>_endpoint_surprise` and `<family>_trend_coherence` (**28** names). Endpoint surprise is the larger absolute departure of either endpoint from the median of the other three periods. Trend coherence is `abs(sum of three signed steps) / sum of their absolute values`, with missing adjacent observations left missing. Neither requires knowing which endpoint is newest.
- `login_count_median4`, `activity_level_median4`, `purchase_frequency_median4`, `skill_rating_median4` (**4**) for cohort matching.
- `login_time_variance_mean4`, `play_pattern_consistency_mean4`, `session_duration_std_mean4`, `performance_stability_mean4`, `reaction_time_std_mean4`, `accuracy_std_mean4` (**6**) and `login_count_cv4`, `activity_level_cv4`, `session_duration_mean_cv4` (**3**) for regularity analysis. The CV features divide within-row standard deviation by absolute within-row mean plus one.
- `login_move_km_12`, `login_move_km_23`, `login_move_km_34`, `login_move_max_km` (**4**) from valid latitude/longitude pairs. Distance is descriptive because period duration and exact login timestamps are unavailable.

All 45 new features are deterministic within a row; no label, ID, cross-row fitted statistic, or external data enters EDA. The 45-feature dataset is independent of the older 91-feature EDA dataset, which remains the frozen v17 source. The notebook's descriptive audit found **265 invalid coordinate pairs masked**. Missingness among the 167 numeric predictors is about 4–5% for typical four-period raw families; these gaps motivate model-local median imputation. `is_anomaly` remains absent.

`src/task5/models/build_model_v7.ipynb` fits two distinct score models on the same **12,000 unlabeled accounts** as the old detector experiment, with an overlapping 10,000-account alternate fit and a separate 4,000-account unlabeled holdout for rank stability. The saved `t5_peer_change_v3` uses five cohort dimensions (`account_age_days` and the four `median4` values) to find 128 nearby accounts, then scores 15 nonnegative change dimensions (14 endpoint surprises plus maximum login displacement) relative to the neighbors' local median and IQR. It averages its three largest positive local deviations. Cohort imputation, scaling, change imputation, and peer statistics are fitted only in the model. Its **top-25% rank-stability Jaccard is 0.85874**. This is noticeably less stable than the original OCSVM's earlier 0.98216 top-25% proxy, though the two stability protocols are not identical; neither is F₃.

The saved `t5_bot_regular_v3` requires both high activity (four-period median login count and activity level) and high regularity (high play-pattern consistency, performance stability, behavior stability; low login-count CV, activity CV, mean login-time variance, mean session-duration standard deviation, mean reaction-time standard deviation). It converts each dimension to a training-fit empirical percentile and takes the **minimum** of mean activity and mean regularity percentile, so either side alone cannot dominate. Its top-25% rank-stability Jaccard is **0.99402**. This score is an interpretable machine-likeness hypothesis, not a verified bot classifier. The model does not rely on security flags.
